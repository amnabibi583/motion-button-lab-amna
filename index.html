Test result:

Motion choices

  • 180ms keeps hover and focus feedback quick and natural.
  • 220ms gives the loading width change enough time to feel intentional.
  • 1.2 seconds keeps success feedback visible without interrupting flow.
  • 360ms makes the error shake noticeable once, without becoming distracting.
  • Transform and opacity are used for smooth, compositor-friendly content motion; only the small button width transition changes layout.
  • Reduced-motion users still receive every color, label, and success/error state, while motion effects are removed.
